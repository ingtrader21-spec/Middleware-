# Search policy

Authorized global search accepts campaign number/code/public ID, lead compact
or full ID, call/callback/transfer/import ID, extension, VICIdial campaign or
lead ID, and external lead alias.

Every result is filtered by the caller's permitted campaign set and field
permissions before rendering. Exact canonical IDs may be globally resolved,
but unauthorized campaign existence is not disclosed. Customer telephone
numbers are excluded from unrestricted search; privileged lookup uses
tokenized/encrypted indexes and audited purpose-bound access.
