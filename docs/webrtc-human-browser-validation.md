# Human browser validation — no telephony

1. Open the approved production desktop URL.
2. Authenticate through canonical Codestra Keycloak.
3. Complete MFA.
4. Grant microphone access.
5. Verify the dashboard, identity bindings, and safe diagnostics.
6. **Do not click Provision + register.**
7. **Do not place a call.**
8. Log out and confirm microphone tracks, sockets, and session data are released.
