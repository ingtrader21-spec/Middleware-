# Middleware Core Gap Analysis (Mission 1)

Evaluates the current repository against the target Middleware Core capabilities. States:
**IMPLEMENTED** (exists, sound, wired, tested) · **PARTIAL** (exists but incomplete) ·
**DUPLICATED** (two or more implementations of the same responsibility) · **MISPLACED**
(exists in the wrong boundary) · **MISSING**. Every PARTIAL entry states what exists, where,
what is missing, why it matters and which Mission fixes it. Mission numbers beyond 2 are the
proposed sequence (2 = define the canonical architecture, 3 = implement the core, 4 = move
adapters and retire duplicates, 5 = certification); Mission 2 may re-sequence them.

Evidence base: `docs/architecture/MIDDLEWARE-BASELINE.md` and
`docs/architecture/MIDDLEWARE-COMPONENT-INVENTORY.md` (baseline `3c675d1…` + this document's commit).

## Structural finding that shapes every row

Two application lineages are mounted by the deployed `integration_api` through `app.router_registry`:

| Lineage | Settings | Runtime | Auth | Storage | Routers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "core" | `app/core/config.py` (88 callers) | `app/entrypoints/runtime.py` | `app/core/jwt_auth.py`, `app/core/auth.py`, `app/core/route_policy.py` | `app/db/models.py` + `app/db/session.py` | `app/api/v1/*`, `app/api/internal/*`, `app/monitoring/*` |
| "Appolon" | `app/config.py` (20 callers) | `app/runtime.py`, `app/appolon_factory.py` | `app/security.py` | `app/storage.py` | `app/control_api.py`, `app/domain_api.py`, `app/webhook_api.py`, `app/compatibility_api.py`, `app/n8n_control_plane.py`, `app/automation_v2.py`, `app/*_adapter.py` (Temporal `ProviderAdapter` shape) |

Neither lineage is wrong on its own; the duplication is the primary architectural debt. Most
DUPLICATED rows below are one entry per lineage.
