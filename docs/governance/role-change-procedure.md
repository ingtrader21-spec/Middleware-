# Governance role-change procedure

Role assignments may be changed only by the mapped Organization Owner or by a
later corporate resolution with equivalent authority.

A role change must:

1. identify the legal entity and authorized assigner;
2. state the exact GitHub login additions and removals;
3. state its effective timestamp;
4. reference checksummed authority evidence;
5. document any role overlap;
6. receive independent exact-head review;
7. merge before the new identity exercises authority;
8. invalidate or supersede earlier mappings explicitly.

No person establishes authority merely by adding their own name to this file.
Unknown or disputed assignments remain inactive.
